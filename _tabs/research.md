My research spans **large language models, reinforcement learning, and optimization**.
My current focus is on **LLM post-training, reasoning agents, and reinforcement learning environments**.
Previously, I studied online learning and optimization algorithms for dynamic pricing and resource allocation problems.
My earlier work provides a theoretical foundation that shapes how I think about AI agents: how they learn from feedback, reason efficiently, and make decisions under uncertainty.
